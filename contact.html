FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, we welcome walk-ins! However, we recommend making reservations, especially for weekend dining, to ensure we can accommodate you at your preferred time.

Is there a dress code?

We maintain a smart casual dress code. We kindly ask that guests refrain from wearing athletic wear, flip-flops, or overly casual attire.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! Please inform your server of any allergies or dietary restrictions, and our kitchen will do their best to accommodate your needs. We offer vegetarian and gluten-free options on our menu.

Is parking available?

Yes, we offer complimentary valet parking on Friday and Saturday evenings. Street parking is available at all other times, and there is a public parking garage within walking distance.

Can I bring my own wine?

We do allow guests to bring their own wine for a corkage fee of $35 per bottle. We kindly ask that you do not bring wines that are already on our list.
